What is Computer Science?

Computers and the Internet are having a great impact on human life and on society. Our lives have become computerized at the speed of light. The Computer Engineering Department offers a unique opportunity for bright young minds to excel in computer science, software, and hardware.

Our Vision, Mission & Principles

Our Vision

  • Develop research projects with international recognition
  • Engage students in globally relevant work

Our Mission

  • Educate students with cutting-edge computer engineering knowledge
  • Provide background for global job opportunities
  • Enrich students with moral and ethical values
  • Develop leadership through social engagement

Our Principles

  • Excellence in teaching and research
  • Loyalty to ethics
  • Professional and dedicated work habits
  • Alertness to technological changes
